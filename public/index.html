About the platform

Mondee Tech Private Limited

Mondee Tech Private Limited is the India arm of Tabhi.ai, consolidating travel inventory worldwide and making it bookable across B2B, B2E and B2B2C. Its mandate inside this platform is narrow and specific: bring the wedding and event vertical onto one stack — bulk-bookable venues, artists with their own storefront, and a single place to search, quote, book and confirm.

Tabhi.ai is the intelligence the group runs on: 23 companies merged into three AI platforms across ten countries, with 65,000+ travel experts, 2M+ hotels and vacation rentals, and 50 million searches a day feeding into the same rails that Mondee, Miraee and Aarna use for events.
